APRS Messenger for UV-K1 / UV-K5 V3

The F4HWN Fusion firmware with background APRS messaging: mailbox, channels, multi-tap typing, optional encryption where it is allowed.

Alpha. Built and tested on a computer; the parts that drive the radio hardware have not run on a radio yet. Transmitting APRS needs an amateur radio licence. Encryption is forbidden on amateur bands; elsewhere follow your country's rules.

Flash it from the browser

  1. Back up the calibration first (once per radio): flash the official Fusion firmware, then dump the calibration in UV Studio and keep the file, named after the radio's serial number.
  2. Connect the radio with a data USB cable and put it into DFU mode; UV Studio shows how.
  3. Open UV Studio with this firmware already loaded, then select Flash Firmware and the radio's serial port.

Chrome, Edge, Opera or Firefox 151+ on a computer (Web Serial). Flashing goes through the radio's bootloader, which is never overwritten; the calibration stays in its own flash sector. Back to stock: restore the calibration file, or use UV Studio's factory reset for the UV-K1 / UV-K5 V3.

After flashing

  1. Menu → a side key setting (F1Shrt … M Long) → MESSAGES.
  2. Press it: enter your callsign, switch the messenger ON.
  3. Tune a VFO to 144.800 MHz FM (IARU Region 1) and leave it there.
